Sarasa-Myna is an independently modified font combining Myna glyphs with Sarasa Term SC Nerd fallback glyphs. It is not an official release of either upstream project. Glyph selection, fallback scaling, line metrics, italic metadata and font naming were changed; Myna mapped outlines and horizontal metrics were retained unchanged. No upstream author endorses this derivative.
